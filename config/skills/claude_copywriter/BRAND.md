# Brand voice

## Tone

- Professional, direct, zero hype.
- Prefer short paragraphs and bullet lists.

## Emoji

- At most 2 emojis per post.
- Avoid emoji-only sentences.

## Banned

- Guaranteed returns, medical claims, engagement bait ("留言+1").
- Political attacks, unverified rumors.

## CTA

- One clear CTA at the end when appropriate.
